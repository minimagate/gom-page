The experiment begins with the [Logical statements v0.1.0 corpus](/data/logical-statements/): 30 controlled statements with canonical versions in five languages and explicit notes on non-complementary antonyms. Each statement is expressed in four related forms—an affirmative proposition, its grammatical negation, a lexical opposite, and the negation of that opposite—to test whether those operations produce distinct and repeatable embedding directions.

The experiment is exploratory and deliberately does not register a hypothesis. Its run-level measurements and interactive visual analysis live in its observation.

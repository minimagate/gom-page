The `logical_statements` corpus contains 30 controlled English base statements and canonical translations in the five project languages. It isolates simple predicates, actions, relations, and quantified propositions so one logical relation can be changed at a time.

The corpus explicitly records cases where an antonym is not a logical complement. Those middle states are part of the measurement problem rather than noise removed from it.
